During the exploratory part of our research project before narrowing down to the fourth down decision, we considered various topic proposals. One such idea we to model the outcomes of run or pass plays in the first three downs. We achieve this by building two separate multinomial logistic regression models  for pass and run plays. This allows us to forecast the entire distribution of outcomes as opposed to predicting a single average gain. Both regression models have the same dependent variable and same predictors and only differ in data samples - one model uses pass plays and the other run plays. In each model, the dependent variable is a categorical variable with 17 categories for no gain or a loss, single-yard gains from 1 to 10, wider bins for longer gains (11–14, 15–19, 20–29 and 30+), a touchdown, and a turnover. The predictors in the models, on the other hand, capture the situational context for each play - field position in 10-yard zones, down, yards to go in groups, and whether the previous play of the drive gained yards and the strength of the two teams.

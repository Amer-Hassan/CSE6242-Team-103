Before you begin

You'll review 12 fictionalized game scenarios based on the project's trained estimates. This is a classroom interface evaluation, not an assessment of your football expertise. Each trial asks which action has the highest estimated win probability, the difference between the two best options, and whether that difference is below 2 percentage points.

No names, emails, IP addresses, or responses are sent to or stored on the application server. Your results stay in this browser until you choose to download a CSV. Avoid refreshing mid-study: progress will be lost. Participation is voluntary; you can stop at any time.

The participant number determines a balanced A/B presentation order and which scenario set uses each interface. To run a pilot, use number 9999 and mark it as a pilot in your own study log; exclude pilot data from the study analysis.

What the ranges mean

An interval shows how the model's estimate changes when historical outcome observations are resampled. It does not cover uncertainty in the trained models or unobserved counterfactuals.

Point estimates are from the main decision engine. Intervals are preliminary and conditional on fixed models; do not interpret them as calibrated 95% confidence intervals.

How to read these results

Go for it

Conversion probabilities from a trained Random Forest; successful gains are averaged across 2022–24 historical outcomes from comparable downs and field positions.

Field goal

Make probability from logistic regression; made-kick outcomes use observed historical post-kickoff possessions, while conventional misses use an approximate kick spot.

Punt

Random Forest predicts the opponent starting position; when the historical punt-outcomes CSV is available, the engine integrates field-position and time variability from 2022–24 residuals. Otherwise it retains the point estimate.

Research prototype. These are scenario-based model estimates, not validated causal estimates or coaching recommendations. Play clocks, scoring, kickoffs and end-of-game states are simplified; kickoff rules evolved after training.
